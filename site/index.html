About this interface & compatibility

Use arrow keys to navigate, OK to select, F to favorite a tile, and Escape / Back to return. TV remote events inside Conjure still require real-device validation.

The TV page is static and read-only. Native commands are available only on the local Mac dashboard. This companion does not install Google Play or replace VIZIO firmware; its server must remain running.

Spotify: open the app on your phone or Mac and check the device picker. Select the TV only if it appears as a Spotify Connect receiver; support is unverified. The Spotify tile opens the official website. TV browser audio and DRM support may vary.

If Conjure cannot display this page, try Mac screen mirroring with AirPlay if the TV appears as a compatible receiver, or use HDMI. For standalone Google Play apps, use a certified Google TV device over HDMI.
